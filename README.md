# Netflix-Data

Based on the analysis of the dataset containing 2,000 records of Netflix users in India, here is a comprehensive study of the data:

1. Subscription & Revenue Overview
The total monthly revenue generated from these users is 749,550 INR, with an average revenue of 374.78 INR per user.

Plan Distribution: The user base is almost equally divided among the four available plans:

Standard: 521 users

Mobile: 496 users

Basic: 494 users

Premium: 489 users

Top Payment Method: Wallet is the most preferred payment method among the users.

2. User Engagement & Preferences
Favorite Genre: Drama is the most popular genre, followed by Comedy and Thriller.

Device Usage: Tablets are surprisingly the most used device for streaming, followed closely by Mobile and Smart TVs.

Subscription Status: Currently, about 48.95% of the users have an Active subscription, while 51.05% have Cancelled their service.

3. Geographical Insights (Top 10 States)
The state of Uttar Pradesh (UP) leads significantly in both user count and revenue generation.

4. Key Takeaways for Data Analysts
Market Balance: The even distribution across subscription plans suggests that Netflix's pricing tiers are well-balanced for the Indian market.

Retention Challenge: With a cancellation rate of over 51%, there is a significant opportunity for the platform to investigate "churn" (why users are leaving) and implement retention strategies.

Regional Dominance: Northern and Western states (UP, Gujarat, Rajasthan) show higher adoption rates compared to others in this specific datase
